### Field 6: What did AI suggest you while building this that you deliberately rejected and why?
- **Format:** Required text paragraph
- **Text to Paste (from `docs/REJECTED_SUGGESTIONS.md`):**

```text
While planning, AI put several options on the table that I deliberately rejected:
1) Paid influencer / YouTuber shoutouts - ₹2,000 cannot buy meaningful reach, and creator followers are general rather than final-years preparing for placement drives. I used the money for capped rewards and a measured ad test.
2) Collecting students' phone numbers for automated WhatsApp blasts - this is spam, risks WhatsApp anti-spam bans, and violates consent and privacy. The plan uses opt-in, human-sent messages by trusted peers in existing groups.
3) Fake urgency such as "only 37 seats left" counters - it is dishonest and hurts credibility with engineering students. I use only a real cohort deadline and authentic live counters.
4) Cash for every referral - invites fraudulent signups with disposable emails and breaks the budget within 100 signups. I used tiered, capped vouchers awarded strictly after attendance verification.
5) WhatsApp Business API bot and a full AI project evaluator in the first 48 hours - too much approval and scope risk. I built one rock-solid referral engine that works and scheduled the project evaluator for "next 24 hours".
```
