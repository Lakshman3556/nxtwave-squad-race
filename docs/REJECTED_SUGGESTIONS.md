# Form Field 6: What did AI suggest you while building this that you deliberately rejected and why?

Paste-ready answer for Google Form Field 6:

---

While planning and architecting this challenge, AI suggested several approaches that I deliberately analyzed and rejected based on growth mathematics, brand safety, and execution constraints:

1. **Paid Influencer / Campus YouTuber Shoutouts:**  
   *AI Suggestion:* Spend ₹1,500 on college meme page shoutouts and micro-influencer stories.  
   *Why I Rejected It:* A ₹1,500–₹2,000 budget cannot purchase meaningful reach on credible creator channels. More critically, follower demographics on general meme pages are broad (1st–3rd years, non-engineering) rather than placement-eligible final-years. I reallocated the budget to high-leverage capped incentives (₹1,000 for top referrers) and a controlled ₹600 ad test.

2. **Bulk Contact Scraping & Automated WhatsApp Blasts:**  
   *AI Suggestion:* Scrape student phone numbers and college WhatsApp group links from LinkedIn/Telegram and automate broadcast messaging.  
   *Why I Rejected It:* Unsolicited bulk messaging violates privacy, causes high report rates that trigger instant WhatsApp phone number bans, and severely damages NxtWave's brand reputation. The plan relies exclusively on opt-in distribution via trusted human champions sharing inside groups they already belong to.

3. **Fake Scarcity & Artificial Countdowns:**  
   *AI Suggestion:* Display false countdowns like "Only 37 seats remaining!" or "14 students viewing this right now" to manufacture FOMO.  
   *Why I Rejected It:* Fake scarcity is dishonest and easily recognized by savvy engineering students, destroying credibility during placement season. I implemented only real deadlines (cohort registration window closes on Day 7) and authentic live counts.

4. **Cash Payouts per Referral:**  
   *AI Suggestion:* Pay students ₹10–₹20 cash via UPI for every friend who signs up.  
   *Why I Rejected It:* Per-referral micro-cash breaks the ₹2,000 budget within 100 signups and actively invites fraudulent self-signups using disposable emails. Instead, I introduced capped vouchers for the top 3 verified referrers (₹500 / ₹300 / ₹200), distributed strictly after workshop attendance is verified.

5. **WhatsApp Business API Chatbot & Automated AI Project Evaluator in Phase 0:**  
   *AI Suggestion:* Build a WhatsApp Business API registration bot and an automated GitHub AI code grader.  
   *Why I Rejected It:* Meta WhatsApp Business verification and template approval can take 48–72 hours and incurs per-conversation API charges—introducing unacceptable operational risk during a 48-hour submission window. An automated AI grader over-scopes the acquisition phase, so I prioritized a rock-solid referral engine and scheduled the code grader as the Day-8 workshop-day completion feature.
